# Doubao Identity and Cross-Medium Art Direction

## Identity Anchors

Doubao is a young, approachable female character. Preserve a combination of anchors rather than relying on one feature:

- small, soft oval face with a rounded jaw;
- large dark-brown eyes with clear pupils and slightly emphasized upper lids;
- neat curved dark-brown brows, small nose, natural thin lips, and a gentle expression;
- warm pale skin adapted to source lighting and palette;
- dark-brown side-parted chin-length bob when the original hairstyle cannot preserve identity;
- slightly oversized head, short neck, narrow shoulders, and compact slender torso.

Do not reduce the eyes or character proportions until identity disappears. Preserve each source figure’s gaze, emotion, age context, pose, and spatial position.

## Hair and Headwear Decision

- Always preserve original hats, crowns, flowers, pins, jewelry, veils, and identity-bearing head ornaments.
- **Strong default:** use Doubao's dark-brown side-parted chin-length bob. Treat this hairstyle as a primary identity anchor, not a fallback of equal weight.
- Preserve the original hairstyle only as a high-confidence exception. At normal viewing size, cover or mentally ignore the hair and confirm that the combined soft oval face, large dark-brown eyes, curved brows, small nose, gentle mouth, slightly large head, short neck, narrow shoulders, and compact body still make the figure unmistakably Doubao.
- Do not preserve original hair merely because it is iconic, historically accurate, attractive, feminine, or useful for distinguishing characters. Those qualities affect integration but do not outweigh Doubao recognition.
- If recognition is borderline, depends on close inspection, reads as a generic woman or anime girl, or relies on the viewer already knowing the task, use the Doubao bob.
- When using the Doubao bob, retain the source hair's period integration through the original medium, lighting, wind, motion, occlusion, and accessories; do not paste a modern glossy hairstyle onto the artwork.
- Naturally refit all original headwear over whichever hairstyle is chosen. Headwear preservation never requires preserving the original underlying hairstyle.
- Either choice must inherit the source medium, environmental color, age, edge treatment, and occlusion.

## Primary Integration Standard

Use `assets/doubao-integration-primary.png` as the strongest example of the intended balance:

- Doubao remains immediately recognizable through the soft oval face, large dark-brown eyes, curved brows, small nose, gentle mouth, and visible dark-brown bob.
- The original blue headscarf, yellow drape, pearl earring, pose, gaze, crop, and role remain intact.
- The bob is fitted beneath the headscarf rather than replacing, covering, or floating above it. Small visible sections at the forehead, temple, cheek, and nape are sufficient to carry the hairstyle identity.
- The face is not a detached clean avatar. Skin, hair, eyes, lips, and headwear share the source painting's directional light, subdued palette, canvas aging, fine craquelure, glazing, and edge hierarchy.
- Doubao's eyes stay readable without becoming glossy modern 3D objects; their highlights, contrast, and sharpness remain controlled by the source medium.
- Preserve the original artwork's emotional restraint. Recognition comes from stable character anchors, not exaggerated expression or excessive facial polish.

Use `assets/doubao-integration-impressionism.png` only to study how the bob and face survive at smaller scale through broken strokes and atmospheric color. ## Medium Adaptation

- **Classical oil:** match glazing, chiaroscuro, skin transitions, canvas, and edge hierarchy without erasing identity.
- **Impressionism/impasto:** use broken strokes and environmental color; allow the face and hair silhouette to remain one clarity level sharper.
- **Watercolor/gouache/pencil:** preserve paper grain, transparent layers, blooms, and dry edges.
- **Ink, woodblock, mural, or traditional painting:** encode identity through the source’s line, flat color, mineral pigment, ink, or carved-mark logic; avoid a Western 3D face.
- **Photography:** match lens, depth, noise, skin, hair, contact shadow, and color temperature; avoid a floating avatar head.

## Failure Repairs

- **Generic anime girl:** strengthen the soft oval face, dark-brown eyes, curved brows, and recognizable hair/body structure together.
- **Generic realistic woman:** restore the slightly large head, short neck, narrow shoulders, and readable eyes.
- **Face-swap effect:** extend reconstruction through neck, shoulders, collar, and upper torso; repair lighting and occlusion.
- **Original hair weakens or merely preserves borderline identity:** replace it with the side-parted chin-length Doubao bob while retaining and refitting all original headwear.
- **Modernized whole image:** lock all non-target regions and restore source texture, color, and edge behavior.
